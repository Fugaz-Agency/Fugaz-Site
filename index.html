Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Fugaz Agency, Amsterdam, is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy covers the information you send us through the Fugaz scan, the contact forms and any direct message you start from this site.

What we collect

Only what you type in yourself: your name, your brand, your website, your email address, your phone number where you give it, the answers you select in the scan, and anything you write in the notes field. We do not buy data, and we do not enrich what you send with third-party sources.

Where it goes

Every submission is delivered to our own email inbox and is read by the two founders. It is used for one purpose: to answer you and to assess whether a working relationship makes sense. It is not sold, rented, published, or shared with advertisers. It is shared with a processor only where that processor is needed to deliver the message to us, our email and hosting providers, and those providers act on our instructions alone.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as it remains relevant to a possible or existing engagement. When we conclude that it no longer serves that purpose, the message and its contents are deleted from our inbox. Where an enquiry stays relevant, an active conversation, a live project, a client relationship, or a record we are legally required to hold, such as an invoice, we retain it for as long as that purpose or that legal obligation lasts, and delete it afterwards. Enquiries that lead nowhere are reviewed and cleared periodically.

Our legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent when you choose to send it, on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services, and, once you become a client, on the performance of our contract with you and on our legal obligations as a registered business.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to our processing, or withdraw your consent at any time. Write to legal@fugaz-agency.com and we will action the request without undue delay and in any case within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Security and changes

Access to the inbox is limited to the founders and protected by account-level authentication. This policy may be updated; the date at the top always reflects the current version.

Questions about this policy: legal@fugaz-agency.com.
